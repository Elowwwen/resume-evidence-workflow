# Reusable Project Material Library

Use for selectable project material across roles, including research, community, creative, operational and technical work.

The evidence bank remains the factual source. Create project modules from confirmed evidence and link each to its source. Record:

- project name, dates and relationship type (employment, internship, part-time, personal or other);
- context and objective;
- personal actions, collaboration and decision process;
- outputs, results, metric scope and evidence status;
- relevant capability angles and previously accepted expressions;
- unresolved facts and claim boundaries.

Load only projects relevant to the current JD. Propose selection, emphasis, compression or expansion based on its requirements. Retain user control over material omissions and mergers unless scoped selection permission already exists. A library entry does not require inclusion in every resume.

Keep internship work under its actual relationship type, avoid duplicating the same work as a personal project, and preserve individual versus team ownership. Update facts in the evidence bank first, then update the linked module; resolve obsolete facts rather than storing contradictions. Store accepted wording in the reuse library when useful, without making a second independent factual record.

Public templates and examples must use blank fields or wholly fictional data.
